# Company Policies and Guidelines

## Remote Work Policy
Full-time employees are eligible for up to two remote workdays per week with prior manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.

## Annual Leave
Employees receive 20 days of paid annual vacation leave per calendar year, accrued monthly. Unused leave up to 5 days may be carried over to the subsequent calendar year.

## Health and Wellness Reimbursement
All full-time staff can claim up to $500 annually for gym memberships, mental health apps, ergonomic home equipment, or fitness classes. Receipts must be submitted by December 15th.

## Technology Equipment
Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Any hardware issues should be reported to the IT Helpdesk via Slack at #it-support or ticket portal.
